Examples

Each example is a genie session that opens public data on the web. Download the session file, then open it in genie: push Ctrl+O (Cmd+O on macOS), or drop the file on the window. genie reads only the parts of the files that the view needs, so the first view can take some seconds.

Exome reads at BRCA1

hg38 at an exon of BRCA1 with ClinVar, phyloP 100-way and the exome of NA12878 from the 1000 Genomes Project, a CRAM file of 8.6 GB on Amazon S3.

brca1-exome.genie

Hi-C on chromosome 21

The whole of chromosome 21 in a Hi-C file of ENCODE (ENCFF080DPJ, version 9). The track menu selects the normalization and observed/expected ratios.

hic-chr21.genie

Structural variants of gnomAD

One megabase of chromosome 21 in gnomAD-SV v4.1, read from Google Cloud Storage: deletions, duplications, insertions, complex rearrangements and breakends.

gnomad-sv.genie

gnomAD with VEP annotations

The variants of gnomAD v4.1 genomes in the APP gene. Cards show the VEP annotations. Open the variant table from the track menu to sort and export them.

gnomad-vep.genie

GWAS in the HLA region

The summary statistics of the GWAS Catalog study GCST90270050 as a Manhattan plot over two megabases of chromosome 6.

gwas-hla.genie

Sashimi plot of ACTB

Strand-specific RNA-seq of the JBrowse demo data on hg19, as a Sashimi plot that shows the junctions with 4 reads or more.

actb-sashimi.genie

Liftover of ClinVar

The ClinVar file for GRCh37, lifted to hg38 with the UCSC chain file, above the ClinVar file for GRCh38 at BRCA1. The variants are at the same places.

clinvar-lift.genie
